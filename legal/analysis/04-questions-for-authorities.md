# Questions for authorities and professionals

Use this as a pre-application agenda. Provide the final top-down plan, component schedule, and a survey or parcel map; request written answers with citations where possible.

## Town of Hardenburgh

1. Please provide or identify the current complete zoning law, amendments, zoning map/status, code-enforcement program, fee schedule, and permit checklist.
2. Which setbacks control: 20/30/50 feet in the posted zoning schedule, 25/25/50 feet in the 2024 permit form, or another amended standard? From what line is the front setback measured for this parcel?
3. What approval and site-plan standards apply to a detached ADU? Does §5.1.1’s hypothetical-subdivision rule apply, and can the shown close compound arrangement qualify?
4. May the 192-square-foot no-kitchen guest cabin be occupied for sleeping as an accessory building? May it include a full bathroom, HVAC, and electricity, and what restrictions apply?
5. How will the bathhouse, connecting decks, outdoor kitchen pavilion, cold plunge, and wood-fired hot tub be classified and permitted?
6. What fire access, driveway, parking, snow, grading, lighting, stream, flood, or steep-slope submissions are required?

## Ulster County / septic and health review

1. Obtain the existing septic permit, design, as-built, maintenance record, approved bedrooms/design flow, and expansion area.
2. Does the final program - principal home, ADU, guest sleeping room, and bathhouse fixtures - fit the approval? If not, what redesign or expansion is feasible?
3. What well-to-septic and water-supply requirements control the selected clearing?

## Surveyor, civil/site professional, and title reviewer

1. Confirm parcel boundaries, area, width, right-of-way, road status, easements, deed restrictions, encroachments, and access.
2. Map contours, elevation, slopes, watercourses, wetlands/buffers, flood zones, existing disturbance, driveway, utilities, septic, and candidate well areas.
3. Test whether each proposed residence could satisfy the zoning law’s hypothetical-lot standard.

## Architect/engineer and code official

1. Confirm the current code editions and occupancy classification of each structure.
2. Establish building separation/fire-resistance implications of the continuous deck network.
3. Define egress, smoke/CO alarms, guards, stairs, snow/wind loads, energy compliance, ventilation, sauna/heater, pool/spa barrier, and outdoor-cooking requirements.
4. Identify which drawings require a New York licensed design professional’s seal and how phase permits should preserve the final master plan.
