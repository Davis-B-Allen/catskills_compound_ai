Dimension-faithful staged site model

Eight orthographic top-down renders of one fixed 3D master model. Each stage only reveals components that have been built by that point.

+Z = north • +X = east • dimensions in feet
Locked geometry: Main tiny home 24×20 (480 sf); bathhouse 16×16; ADU 22×20 (440 sf); guest cabin 16×12. The bathhouse is rotated on site so its sauna/shower façade faces east into the courtyard: sauna glass occupies the northern portion of that courtyard-facing wall and the shower portion remains solid. The cold plunge sits immediately east of the southern/shower portion; the wood-fired hot tub is south and slightly west of the plunge. The ADU and guest cabin are shifted slightly north and east, the firepit is re-centered within the overall building group, and the completed deck is one continuous rectilinear circulation system enclosing the courtyard, with generous platforms around the bathhouse, spa tubs, ADU, guest cabin, and outdoor kitchen.
