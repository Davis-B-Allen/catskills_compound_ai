# Interesting Potential Todos

This document captures interesting ideas and potential improvements for the Catskills compound project that could be explored in future phases.

## Ideas

1. Search the internet for template floor plans for small homes and ADUs, review them for cost and estimated build cost, then update the master site plan based on the choice.

2. Choose explicit floor plans from among a variety of options for all remaining structures on the property, then redo the proposal starting from the 3D site plan and include new sections for each individual structure and its floor plan.

3. Do more thorough research into contracting and building costs and typical cost of home construction in the region in order to build a more accurate estimate of total project cost.
