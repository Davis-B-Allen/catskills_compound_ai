## Implementation strategy

### Before land purchase

- Obtain the deed/tax map, current boundary and topographic survey, title/easement review, and road/right-of-way status.
- Obtain the existing septic approval, design, as-built, maintenance record, approved bedrooms/design flow, and reserve area.
- Confirm potable-water feasibility and utility capacity; do not treat a seasonal stream as a residential water source.
- Screen flood zones, wetlands, protected streams and buffers, elevation, steep slopes, watershed requirements, and existing clearing limits.
- Present the full Stage 8 concept to Hardenburgh for written pre-application feedback.
- Refresh land, site-work, design, permit, utility, structure, and contingency costs.

### Before Stage 1 construction

- Translate the conceptual model into surveyed parcel coordinates.
- Lock access, grading/drainage, septic/well, utility service, fire access, snow storage, and the final building/deck envelopes.
- Decide the foundation and utility strategy for every later phase.
- Install approved sleeves, capped lines, and spare conduit during early trenching where doing so avoids future disturbance.
- Create a phase plan that allows each occupied component to receive its required certificate before use.

### During every stage

1. Reconfirm source assumptions and permit scope.
2. Update the master geometry before producing beauty images.
3. Review Stage 8 for downstream conflicts, then review the immediate stage.
4. Record actual cost, product, permit, and as-built information as new sources.
5. Update the affected proposal sections and rebuild the assembled document.

### Procurement posture

Use contractor-led delivery where sequencing, inspection, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, septic, well, or life-safety risk is high. Reserve owner-performed work for scopes the owner can execute legally, safely, and without undermining warranties, schedule, or inspection access. The owner-builder budget is a strategy, not an assumption that licensed work can be self-performed.
