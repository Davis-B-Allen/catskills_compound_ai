## Stage 5 - Courtyard seating and hot/cold bathing

Add six seating positions around the firepit, the compact cold plunge east of the bathhouse’s shower-side wall, and the wood-fired hot tub south and slightly west of the plunge. Preserve the sauna glazing and keep both vessels integrated with one enveloping bathhouse deck.
