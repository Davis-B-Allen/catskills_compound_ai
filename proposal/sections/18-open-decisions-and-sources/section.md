### Caveat

This proposal is a living feasibility and visualization document. Actual legality, permitting, site placement, title, septic capacity, well location, structural design, energy compliance, fire safety, grading, drainage, utility service, construction cost, schedule, insurance, and tax treatment require current confirmation from the relevant authorities and qualified professionals. Dimension-controlled model outputs express site-plan intent; photorealistic images express atmosphere and material intent.
