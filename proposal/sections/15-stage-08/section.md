## Stage 8 - Outdoor kitchen and final deck links

Complete the east side with a covered approximately 18 x 16 ft open-air kitchen/bar pavilion. Its open face points west toward the courtyard and includes grill, smoker, prep counters, bar seating, lighting, and outlets. Final broad deck links close the circulation loop without moving earlier elements.
