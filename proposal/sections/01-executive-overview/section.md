## Executive overview

### Goal

Acquire a secluded, buildable Catskills parcel and develop a low-rise vacation compound in deliberate phases. The final arrangement gathers several small structures around a mostly flat firepit courtyard, with a continuous deck system providing circulation and outdoor living.

### Final concept

- A 24 x 20 ft principal compact home anchors the north edge.
- A 16 x 16 ft bathhouse sits to the west of the courtyard with sauna, shower, bathing, and toilet functions.
- A cold plunge and wood-fired hot tub join the bathhouse deck.
- A 22 x 20 ft full ADU adds an independently livable dwelling to the southeast.
- A 16 x 12 ft no-kitchen guest sleeping cabin fills the south-western gap.
- A covered 18 x 16 ft outdoor kitchen/bar closes the east side.
- A fixed central firepit and a clean, contiguous deck network tie the composition together.

### Development thesis

The entire Stage 8 layout should be validated before construction begins. Stage 1 can then establish the correct home location, utility capacity, access, finished-floor datum, and future trench routes. Later stages become additive projects rather than expensive corrections.

### Immediate decision gates

1. Verify that a candidate parcel can support the full occupancy, water, septic, access, and resource constraints.
2. Obtain written municipal feedback on the detached ADU, guest sleeping cabin, bathhouse, tubs, pavilion, and close compound layout.
3. Reconcile the conceptual geometry to an actual survey and topographic/site plan.
4. Refresh costs and decide which work is contractor-led, owner-built, or deferred.
