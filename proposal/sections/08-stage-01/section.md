## Stage 1 - Principal compact home

Build the approximately 24 x 20 ft / 480 sf principal dwelling first. Include a kitchen, full bathroom, flexible living/sleeping area, heat pump, full utilities, and service capacity sized for later bathhouse and ADU loads.
