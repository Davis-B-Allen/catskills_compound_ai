The 16 x 16 ft bathhouse is the most developed interior concept and should not be casually reoriented. In the accepted site model, its **east** sauna/shower facade faces the firepit courtyard. Sauna glazing occupies the northern part of that facade; the southern shower portion remains substantially solid. The main entry is on the opposite/back side and is reached by the wraparound deck.

At Stage 5, the cold plunge sits immediately east of the shower-side wall. The larger wood-fired hot tub sits south and slightly west of the plunge, overlapping the bathhouse in east-west dimension. The deck should envelop the building and both tubs.

The plan reserves sauna, shower, soaking tub, vanity/toilet, service space, and south entry. Heater model, clearances, guards, floor/drainage assembly, waterproofing, freeze protection, mechanical ventilation, plumbing load, glazing safety, egress, and pool/spa protections remain professional design tasks.
