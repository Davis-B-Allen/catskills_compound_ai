## Component program

| Component | Intended program | Design boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Principal compact home | Approx. 480 sf with kitchen, full bath, living/sleeping space, heat pump, and full utilities | Establishes legal principal residence and service capacity; not a code-defined ≤400 sf tiny house. |
| Deck network | Phased outdoor circulation and living surface | Must remain contiguous, drain safely, handle snow, and preserve code-required guards, stairs, and building relationships. |
| Firepit courtyard | Flat gravel/stone pad, stone ring, later seating and low lighting | Avoid sunken construction; confirm fire restrictions, clearances, and insurance. |
| Bathhouse | 256 sf with sauna, shower, soaking tub, vanity/WC, and service zone | No sleeping or kitchen; plumbing, sauna, ventilation, egress, and occupancy require professional/code review. |
| Hot/cold bathing | Wood-fired hot tub and compact cold plunge | Integrate with bathhouse deck without blocking glazing; plan barriers/covers, bonding, drainage, and heater clearances. |
| Detached ADU | Approx. 440 sf complete dwelling with kitchen, bath, HVAC, and utilities | Approval path and relation to multiple-principal-building rule must be confirmed in writing. |
| Guest cabin | Approx. 192 sf no-kitchen sleeping building with small bath, HVAC, power, egress, and alarms | Treat as occupied permitted work; do not rely on a shed exemption. |
| Outdoor kitchen/bar | Covered open-air grill, smoker, prep, bar seating, light, and power | No sleeping use; confirm gas/solid-fuel, structure, electrical, and fire requirements. |

The component list defines intent, not fixed construction systems. Foundation type, utility strategy, structural framing, envelope assemblies, and equipment should be selected after site and code validation.
