## Regulatory and approval path

The legal concept remains plausible but unresolved. The current working framing is one principal dwelling, a detached ADU pursued through the route identified by Ulster County, a permitted no-kitchen accessory sleeping cabin, and accessory wellness/outdoor-living structures.

### Findings that shape the plan

- Hardenburgh’s posted law uses one town-wide residential district and lists basic dimensional standards, but natural-resource rules can materially change capacity and placement.
- More than one principal residential building must satisfy area, bulk, and subdivision rules as if separate lots were created.
- Ulster County’s current guide says Hardenburgh ADUs are allowed by conditional-use permit, while the posted older town PDFs do not expressly define that route.
- The local zoning schedule and 2024 permit form show conflicting front/side setback numbers.
- The guest cabin cannot rely on the optional ≤144 sf shed/playhouse permit-exemption concept: it is 192 sf and intended for sleeping.
- The 480 sf principal home and 440 sf ADU are not “tiny houses” under the 2025 New York code’s ≤400 sf definition.
- Hot tubs, spas, and other vessels capable of holding more than 24 inches of water fall within current residential-code pool/spa provisions.

Until resolved, reserve a conservative 25 ft front, 30 ft side, and 50 ft rear working envelope, but do not treat it as a zoning determination.
