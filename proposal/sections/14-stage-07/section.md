## Stage 7 - Guest sleeping cabin and deck

Add the 16 x 12 ft / 192 sf guest cabin between the bathhouse and ADU. The intended version has no kitchen but includes a compact bathroom, electricity, heating/cooling, appropriate emergency escape, and smoke/CO protection.
